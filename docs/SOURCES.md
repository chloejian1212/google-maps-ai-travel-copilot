## How the sources were used

The case study is an edited account based on the designer's own reflections and the visible artifacts. Assignment instructions, unused answer placeholders, and the quotation in the course template are excluded from the portfolio narrative.

The five sketch images were extracted directly from embedded images in the PDF, preserving their original image pixels. The original submission PDF is not redistributed in this repository; it includes the course assignment template alongside the student's work.

## Claims deliberately limited

- The source does not give a bibliographic reference for its statement about Gen Z travel preferences. No demographic statistic is claimed here.
- No independent assessment of current Google Maps features was performed. Statements about the design opportunity refer to the student's concept, not to a verified product comparison.
- No measured usability results or production implementation are supplied.
- The project name used here, **Google Maps AI Travel Copilot**, is a descriptive portfolio title based on the concept, not an official Google product name.
