# Design decisions

| Decision | Source basis | Intended role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Embed the copilot in a familiar map context | The designer's reflection and mobile sketches | Reduce the conceptual jump between looking at a place and planning a trip |
| Offer map-based and list-based place selection | Original route sketches | Explore different ways to gather destinations before creating a route |
| Ask for a travel period | Route and travel-guide sketches | Give the planning request a clear time frame |
| Keep the route visible on a map | Sketches and route-map mockups | Help users connect an itinerary to its locations |
| Connect planning to a shareable travel guide | Reflection, guide sketches, sharing and itinerary mockups | Extend the experience from preparation to revisiting and sharing a trip |
| Refine short English interface copy | The designer's AI-use reflection | Support concise, consistent wording with ChatGPT assistance |

These are design intentions drawn from the supplied work. No before-and-after usability result is attached to them.

## Follow-up design work

A future iteration should specify editable route constraints, loading and error states, the limits of generated information, and privacy choices before sharing. The current artifacts do not establish production behavior for these cases.
